official ielts writing band calculation & descriptors

official rounding protocol:
• arithmetic mean of 4 criteria (TA/TR, CC, LR, GRA)
• if average ends in .25 → rounds up to .5 (e.g. 6.25 → 6.5)
• if average ends in .75 → rounds up to next whole band (e.g. 6.75 → 7.0)
• other increments round to nearest 0.5 (e.g. 7.125 → 7.0, 7.375 → 7.5)
